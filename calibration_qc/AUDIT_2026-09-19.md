## Findings that block a no-reshoot conclusion

1. **Station assignments are provisional.** `merge_v3.py` guesses stations using old polynomial maps and refits maps to those guesses. Its 46/59 matches and fitting residuals are not independent validation. Partial-board centroids also shift with which corners are visible. Board corner IDs identify positions on the board; they do not independently identify the board's station in the field.
2. **The newer nearest-cone assignment also needs review.** `label_placements.py` uses the lowest visible ChArUco ID as an origin proxy. Even ID 0 is an internal corner, not the outer board origin; when it is occluded the proxy changes. ID 0 is present in only 224/364 CH01 and 82/246 CH02 candidate frames. Time-only clustering can merge different placements. Do not treat its labels as measured truth.
3. **Per-camera independent tests are not demonstrated.** The provisional visibility report gives CH01 18 training / 5 validation / 6 final-test stations and CH02 16 / 3 / 4 at the maximum-corner threshold. These counts are both assignment-dependent and single-best-frame based. They do not meet or prove the planned minimum of 12 independent final-test positions per camera. The exact shortages are not reliably 6 and 8 because station labels remain unsettled.
4. **Ground pose and height remain unverified.** The operator procedure recorded in the README is origin on cone centre and cones on cord ticks. Confirm the specific physical board origin, long-edge direction, board thickness and actual support height. A board above or tilted relative to the target plane cannot simply be assigned z=0. Use settled frames; do not equate low pixel motion with a ground pose.
5. **Use the actual September imperial station grid.** The scripts use training x = 24,96,168,240,312,384,456 inches and y = 12,66,120,174,228 inches; validation/test x = 60,132,204,276,348,420 and y = 39,93,147,201 inches. This differs from the earlier proposed metric grid. Convert actual surveyed coordinates by 2.54 and preserve the original units; do not silently reuse the old proposed coordinates.

The README correction explicitly says T33, T43 and F63 WERE placed. Their absence from an automatic assignment is not evidence they were skipped. Do not request those exact reshoots solely from the old missing-station list.

## Recommended short supplement if access remains available

1. Preserve current camera position, orientation, image transforms and stitching settings. If moved since September 18, a new capture must be treated as a different calibration configuration; it cannot validate the old configuration without a separate registration/consistency assessment.
2. Record at least **12 new, independently measured final-test positions visible in each priority camera**, distributed through its intended valid area. Include near/middle/far ground, at least three edge positions and three positions adjacent to usable seams; edge/seam categories may overlap. Shared positions can serve both cameras when both can see the same grounded marks. Do not insist on points inside an ambiguous stitch band: declare that band invalid.
3. At every position record a unique ID, actual origin coordinates, board-axis direction from a second measured reference, target-plane/support height, camera visibility, and PC start/end time. Hold 10–15 seconds with people clear. Check at least three settled frames per camera, with 12 distributed corners over three rows/columns. A measured thin cross/line intersection with manual pixel annotation is a fallback where the board cannot be resolved.
4. Keep these new final-test positions out of fitting and parameter selection. Add separate training/validation positions only where verified existing coverage has a gap, especially around boxes, poles, edges and either side of unusable seams. Do not consume final tests to fill training gaps.
5. Verify one closed clip on site before packing up. Correct station logs and plane contact matter more than collecting hundreds of additional frames at the same location.

This is a risk-reducing supplement, not a claim that all 12 positions per camera are definitely missing. If verified existing independent tests meet the requirement, retain those and reduce new collection to confirmed gaps. Allow setup/measurement time; short holds alone are not the total acquisition time.

## Recovery path if collection has ended

Recover station identities from operator records plus a synchronized multi-camera timeline. Review origin and axis alignment at each settled placement. Preserve station-level training/validation/test separation across frames and cameras. Fit candidate ground models only after these correspondences are trusted; assess folds, support coverage and seams, then report centimetre errors per held-out station. Reduce valid regions where evidence is insufficient. Do not relabel final tests from the fitted model to improve the score.

The recorded NVR OSD offset is approximately PC minus 59 minutes 25 seconds for this session. Keep segment/PC timestamps and visible OSD timestamps explicitly separate during annotation rather than applying an assumed one-hour shift.
