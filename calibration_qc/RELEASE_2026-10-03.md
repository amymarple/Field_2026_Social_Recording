# Release 2026-10-03: ray-space correction

`paddock_map.load()` now returns `RayCamera`s built from `ray_correction.json` (next to `camera_fit.npz`, here and in
`<root>\qc\`; bound to the fit by its sha256, `6e4b54e9...`). The bundle is unchanged. The previous release, 10-02b
(bundle + the object-space ground warp `frame_correction.json`, sha `2654a6a6...`, unchanged), stays available as
`load(rays=None)`; `load(correct=False)` is the raw bundle as before. Accepted by the operator 2026-10-03.

## What changed

Instead of warping the ground point a camera's ray hits (one xy shift whatever the height), each camera's RAY is
corrected (`raymap.py`, AUDIT_FABLE_SOTA_2026-10-03.md recommendation 1): per pano a degree-4 polynomial in azimuth /
elevation plus an offset per lens half, blended over a 3 deg linear band at the stitch seam; per pinhole degree-4
extra distortion terms in normalised coordinates; per camera a centre shift. Fitted by `refit_rays.py --deg 4
--sig-coef 0.05 --walltop --wt-w 1.0 --seam-w-deg 3 --no-tape-dist` on:

* the release's ground labels: 09-18 cones (latent points held 4 in to the lattice), T cords and wall feet; 09-30
  cones and Y cords carried to the 09-18 pixels (`session_2026-09-30_drift_final.json`);
* camera centres: the lenses triangulated from 167 clicks in drone frames (`DRONE_LENS_TRI_2026-10-03.txt`; every
  pair's distance at the ChArUco plate's scale, sigma 15 mm + 0.5 %, and every pair's height DIFFERENCE, sigma 20 mm)
  and the operator's taped heights (sigma 30 mm; the drone's floor runs on the grass tops, 78 mm above the tape's
  datum, so only the tape gives absolute heights). The tape's horizontal distances are NOT used: the operator notes
  that a long tape run sags and bends, and only rigid objects (the ChArUco plate, the houses) are exact. They differ
  from the drone's by 0.4-3.0 in; with them in the fit (sigma 30 mm) every check below changes by <= 3 mm.
* above the ground: the operator's 09-18 wall-top polylines in CH01-CH04 (analysis repo, `cv/configs/landmarks/2026c`)
  meet each wall's design plane at the drone's wall-top height there (`DRONE_WALLTOP_2026-10-03.txt`). The wall
  shape is stable: 09-04 and 09-18 labels in CH03 agree along the west wall to +-3 px after the camera's own motion.

## What it does not show

1. **Above the ground only where wall-top data are.** With the west wall held out of the fit (step-seam variant C,
   README 2026-10-03) the west wall top stays 90-180 mm high: the correction is local in the image and three walls do
   not carry to the fourth. The wall-top agreement is a fit, not a validation. For an animal the relevant height
   (60-105 mm) is checked by the ball.
2. **CH01 | CH04 got worse (45 -> 85 mm, 4 cells at the east end).** The ball's clock is the weak part there
   (RELEASE_2026-10-02.md); still-ball spots would settle it.
3. **The stitch seam.** A hard switch between the two lens halves (candidate D) left a 24-26 cm fold at u = W / 2;
   a linear blend over 1, 3, 6 or 10 deg removes it and scores the same on every check (within 1 mm), so the band
   width is not determined by these data; 3 deg was taken. The vendor stitch runs at its default (Distance 8 m;
   Reolink support, "How to Set up Image Stitching").
4. **The ground is z = 0.** The drone shows the paddock floor within +-5 cm (sd 3 cm) of a plane; the release maps
   onto the plane, so a rat in a 5 cm hollow on a pano reads ~4 cm off (0.8 mm per mm of height).
5. **Scale.** The camera distances are the drone's at the plate's scale (1.2 % below the design pole grid); the plate
   and the houses are the only exact references (cone and cord positions and long tape runs are not).
6. **Other days.** Camera motion after 09-18 is the analysis repo's per-day correction (`cv_field/frame_correction.py`
   `Corrections.to_paddock`), which calls this `load()`.

## API notes

Same methods. `to_paddock_inv` / `sees` invert the corrected rays numerically (Newton on the pixel, exact to 1e-11
mm). For the pinholes `homography(z)` and `undistort()` are the corrected camera's (they agree with `to_paddock`
exactly); `dist()` is still the bundle's lens only, so `cv2.undistort(img, K, dist)` does NOT include the
correction - use `undistort()` on points. `correction` is None (no ground warp); the verified support is the 10-02b
polygon carried into the physical frame (`support_in`, inches).
