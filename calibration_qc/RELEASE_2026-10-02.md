Adopted by the operator on 2026-10-02 after the desk experiment both audits proposed
(`AUDIT_ASTRA_2026-10-02.md` Experiment B, `AUDIT_FABLE_2026-10-02.md` E1(a)).

## What changed

Only `frame_correction.json` (sha256 `986676ccddaa73b6...`); the bundle `camera_fit.npz` is the 2026-09-24 one,
byte for byte (sha256 `6e4b54e9264a3675...`). Copies: `calibration_qc/` and the data root `<root>\qc\`. Previous
warps: `<root>\qc\release_2026-10-01\` (git `9e5486a`) and `<root>\qc\release_2026-09-24\` (git `c3b5cef`).

The 10-01 warp fitted each camera's 2026-09-18 cone labels to the DESIGN position of their station, exactly; the
design lattice is not where the cones were (the cameras jointly put the cones ~10 cm off design, median), and
cameras that see different cones were pulled toward different wrong targets. Now every 2026-09-18 station seen
by the cameras is one shared unknown position, held to its design station by a 4 in prior, like the 2026-09-30
supplement cones; the T cords and the straight wall foot stay exact lines and fix the frame. The ball sweep is
NOT in the fit (with it the boards got worse, 46 vs 39 mm); it is a check (`ball_check.py`).

Reproduce (from `calibration_qc`, data root on the portable drive or `G:\calibration`):

    python refit_supplement.py --session F:\calibration\session_2026-09-30_15-49-39 --out <dir> ^
        --deg CH03=2,CH04=2,CH05=2,CH06=2 --soft-lattice 4 --rel <root>\qc\release_2026-09-24\frame_correction.json

`--rel` pins the 09-24 warp as starting point and baseline (the default would be the current release).

## Known limits and open points

1. **Absolute frame unverified.** Cameras now agree better with each other, but the frame is fixed only by the
   cords, the wall foot and the cones' average; the cones sit ~10 cm off design where the cameras see them. Only
   the pole / house survey (`PRETEARDOWN_SURVEY_SHEET_2026-10-01.html`) can say where the lattice really is.
2. **The CH04 end** stays the weak handoff: boards CH02-CH04 76 mm (systematic dy +52 mm), balls CH0x-CH04 ~250
   mm with fitted clocks. The ball clock for CH04 (+0.74 s) disagrees with the burnt-in clocks (CH04 within
   0.1 s of CH03); the still-ball spots and the clock event on the survey sheet are meant to settle it.
3. **Selection.** The variant was chosen by looking at the boards (soft prior 2/4/8, with or without balls).
   The boards stay the independent check of the warp, but not of this choice. The other two readings: the
   held-out cones (66 vs 61 mm, slightly worse than 10-01) and the balls (81 mm, against 75 for the 10-01 warp
   that was fitted on them).
4. **No bundle-independent number.** `cv_folds_eval.py` refits the 09-24 warp method per fold and cannot score
   this one; `CV_FOLDS.txt` (76 / 137 mm) stays a 09-24 number. `DOWNSTREAM_VALIDATION.txt` 1(a) is valid
   (boards: 38 / 84 / p95 96 mm); its ensemble sections compare against the 09-18-only method and are not.
5. **Cohort footage: the day/night (IR <-> colour) chain exists** in the analysis repository
   (`Field2026_Social_analysis`, plan `implementation_plan/2026-09-28-cohort3-camera-stability.md` revision 3 and
   the operator's decisions of 2026-10-02): CH01/CH02 nights by the dusk hand-off chain with a dawn closure check
   (`landmark_night.py`; 11/14 and 13/14 nights pass at <= 3 px), CH03/CH04 from the operator's 09-04 day (12:00)
   and night (03:01) references tied to 09-18, at ~5-10 px. The two audits did not see it (section 3 of both). The
   chain ends in the 09-18 reference frames, which are IR; the boards were shot in colour for CH01/CH03/CH04. For
   the panos the two modes differ by < 2.5 px; for CH03/CH04 the operator's colour/IR switch test settles it.
6. **Height.** Validated near the ground only; downstream needs a fixed keypoint height (both audits, section 6).
