# Release 2026-10-01: ground correction refitted with the 2026-09-30 cones and ball sweep

**Superseded by `RELEASE_2026-10-02.md`.**

Written for an independent audit. Adopted by the operator on 2026-10-01 after the comparison below.

## What changed

Only the per-camera ground warp, `frame_correction.json`. The bundle `camera_fit.npz` (poses, lens models) is
byte-identical to the 2026-09-24 release (sha256 `6e4b54e9264a3675...`), so `paddock_map.load()` accepts the new
warp (it is sha-bound to the bundle).

Model: `refit_supplement.py` refits the six warps jointly (soft-L1, f_scale 3 in, ridge 8 in per term, warp degree
3 for CH01/CH02 as in the release, 2 for CH03-CH06) on:

* the 2026-09-18 ground labels, as in the release (cones on stations, T cords, wall foot);
* 105 observations of 58 cones labelled on 2026-09-30, each a tie point held to its design position by a 4 in prior;
* the long cords Y39 / Y201 as lines, weight 0.5;
* the ball sweep: every 2-s step at which two or more cameras saw the ball (100 steps, 236 marks) as a free tie
  point, the ball centre 105 mm above the ground, one clock offset per camera (CH02 = reference), weight 0.5.

2026-09-30 pixels are carried into the 2026-09-18 pixel frame by each camera's landmark-drift similarity
(`session_2026-09-30_landmark_drift.json`, CH05/CH06 unmoved). Ball marks: `session_2026-09-30_ball_labels.json`
(the operator's drawings plus machine marks the operator accepted; unchecked machine marks are excluded).

Reproduce (data root on the portable drive or `G:\calibration`):

    cd calibration_qc
    python refit_supplement.py --session F:\calibration\session_2026-09-30_15-49-39 --out <dir> ^
        --deg CH03=2,CH04=2,CH05=2,CH06=2 --balls session_2026-09-30_ball_labels.json --ball-weight 0.5

## Known limits and open points (please check these)

1. **The balls add little beyond the cones.** On held-out blocks of the sweep the ball term gives 88 vs 89 mm; it
   helps the held-out cones (61 vs 69) and the in-sample balls. At ball weight 1 the boards get worse (54 / 109),
   at 0.3 they are unchanged (51 / 108). Weight 0.5 was chosen by looking at these same checks (0.3 / 0.5 / 1), so
   there is mild selection on the evaluation data.
2. **The CH04 end (x > 400 in) is the weak handoff:** balls CH0x-CH04 still 20-29 cm apart. Both sweep passes
   through CH04's view ran in -y, so CH04's clock offset (+0.8 s fitted) and a y offset cannot be told apart; CH04's
   own landmark-drift transform is uncertain (two 09-30 frames disagree by 14 px / 0.5 deg).
3. **Model ceiling.** The boards enter the bundle and still disagree by 52 mm between cameras, against a corner
   detection noise of a few mm: the camera models cannot represent the cameras better, so more ground points of
   the same kind will not help much. Plumb lines (`session_2026-09-18_pole_check.txt`): pano pole edges bend
   4-10 px and stand 0.5-4 deg off vertical; CH03's corner poles 10-19 deg. The Duo 3 panoramas are two stitched
   lenses fitted with one model.
4. **Height.** Everything is fitted at ground level (boards 6 mm, cones 50 mm, ball 105 mm). The panos move a point
   2.3-2.5 mm sideways per mm of height error at the far end; above the ground the release is not validated.
5. **Stale outputs, not recomputed for this release:** `CV_FOLDS.txt`, `CV_LANDMARKS.txt`, `FOLD_STABILITY.txt`
   (fold experiments of the 09-24 pipeline); `CALIBRATION_REPORT_2026-09-24_rev2.md` (its held-out placement
   accuracy, 76 / 137 mm, is for the old warp); `DOWNSTREAM_VALIDATION.txt` sections 1(b) onwards - its ensemble
   is refitted from the 09-18 labels only, so it now measures the distance between this release and the old
   method (bin flip 59 %, seam jump 75 mm) rather than the spread of legitimate calibrations; it needs an
   ensemble built from `refit_supplement.py` variants. `CALIBRATION_FIT.txt` / `fit_manifest.json` describe the
   unchanged bundle.
6. Each camera's verified support polygon now also covers its ball marks, so `to_paddock` returns positions
   slightly further out than before.
7. Ball centres: SAM 3 marks reviewed by the operator; an outline-fitting refinement was tried and rejected
   (`session_2026-09-30_ball_refine_check.txt`). The ball-centre height of 105 mm was checked: cameras agree best
   for an assumed 80-105 mm.
