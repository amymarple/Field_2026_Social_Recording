# Extra In-Box Sleep Cameras — CH07 / CH08 (separate recorder)

## Date

2026-07-07. Change is currently uncommitted.

## Why

CH05/CH06 view the shelter **through an IR-transmitting window**, and fog/condensation on
that glass has proven hard to solve — degrading sleep observation inside the home box. Two
extra single-lens cameras were placed **inside the box** to record sleep directly (no glass in
the path). They are recorded as **CH07/CH08** alongside the Reolink footage so the existing QC,
watchdog, and copy tooling pick them up automatically.

## What Changed

- Added `reolink_record/extra_cam_record.ps1` — a SEPARATE continuous recorder (clone of
  `thermal_record.ps1`) for two direct-IP (Dahua-format) cameras, writing hourly fragmented MP4
  into `E:\Reolink_record\CH07` / `CH08`.
  - Own mutex `Global\ReolinkExtraCamRecorder`, own log `extra_cam_recorder.log`.
  - **Never blanket-kills ffmpeg** and **retention/disk-guard are scoped to CH07/CH08 only**, so
    it cannot touch the Reolink (CH01-06) or thermal recorders, or WISER.
- Added `reolink_record/install_extra_cam_task_system.ps1` — registers SYSTEM task
  **"Field Extra Cam Recorder (CH07-CH08)"** (at-startup + self-heal every 5 min).
- Config `E:\Reolink_record\extra_cam.config.psd1` (creds, off-repo): CH07 = `192.168.1.110`,
  CH08 = `192.168.1.111`, RTSP `cam/realmonitor?channel=1&subtype=0`, admin/<password> (creds in off-repo config, not in git).
- **`recording_health_check.ps1`**: restricted the NVR-reboot exception `GroupPattern` from
  `^CH\d{2}$` to `^CH0[1-6]$`. CH07/CH08 are direct cameras (not through the NVR), so a Sunday-
  14:00 gap on them is a REAL fault and must not be excused as an NVR reboot.
- No change needed to the copy scripts or the recording-alive watchdog — they auto-discover
  `CH*` and already include CH07/CH08 (verified).

## Cameras / streams

Both are single-lens **2560×1440 HEVC** (`channel=1` main): CH07 ~3.3 Mbps @ 20 fps,
CH08 ~3.0 Mbps @ 30 fps (~2.6 GB/day combined). The cameras' `channel=2` (thermal) returns
**403 Forbidden** — they are visual-only for this use. Networking: `.110` on the IP-Camera
segment, `.111` on the WISER segment (reachable via per-segment ARP; WISER config untouched).
